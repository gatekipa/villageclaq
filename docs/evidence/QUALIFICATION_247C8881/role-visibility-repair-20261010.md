## Continuity and remaining decisions

The canonical transfer RPC prevents direct owner assignment and sole-owner exit; multiple admin rows are supported and the newly installed role guard makes owner the only current role-grant authority. The installed guard currently breaks the second update in the atomic transfer: after demoting the old owner there is briefly no active owner, and target promotion raises `OWNER_ROLE_CHANGE_REQUIRED`, rolling back the transaction. The installed `invitation_manager_read` also lets a former inviter read invitation rows after exit because that inviter branch omits an active-membership check. Any active admin can currently direct-update their own membership `group_id` into another group without changing `role`, retaining admin authority there; this is established by the live policy/trigger definitions, not by an exploit attempt. The active owner and records remain intact, but transfer is unavailable until the separate prepared correction is applied and verified on a fictional succession fixture. Two-/three-admin appointment also needs its own fixture before PASS. For an unavailable/deceased owner, the recommended policy is a verified claim with identity/legal evidence, two independent platform approvers, notice/challenge window, and immutable audit; no automatic inactivity transfer. Jude must approve the responsible authority and evidence standard before implementation.

The canonical lifecycle RPC preserves membership, debt, payment, minute and audit rows and prevents an owner from exiting without transfer. Active-membership checks deny a departed officer immediately. The unclosed governance gaps are: exiting does not currently end that officer's assignment row, so a one-holder role can block a successor; the legacy `positions_select` policy calls `get_user_group_ids()`, which lists exited memberships and still lets a former member read role definitions; direct authorized role/position DML currently has no comprehensive database audit trigger; and the installed owner-role guard blocks the second step of the canonical atomic ownership-transfer RPC after the old owner is demoted. `20261010050000_founder_governance_followup.sql` is a reviewable **unapplied** follow-up: on future active→inactive transitions it would set assignment `ended_at`, preserve the row and audit the handover; it would also replace `positions_select` with an active-member read policy, add row-level audits for membership-role and position authority changes, permit only the canonical flagged transfer promotion while the verified old owner is the active demoted admin, and require active membership for the inviter read branch without changing recipient identity reads, require owner authority to revoke privileged invitations, and make membership tenant identity immutable while preserving the canonical proxy-claim transition. No stale active assignments existed in the isolated readback. Applying this additional migration and testing separate departure, succession, and foreign-group fixtures require fresh authorization; the owner's approval here expressly covered only the disclosed first migration.

Outstanding former-member obligations remain in historical reports; future scheduled obligations select active memberships under the existing producer policy. Those behaviors were inspected in source, but a separate exit fixture was not run.
