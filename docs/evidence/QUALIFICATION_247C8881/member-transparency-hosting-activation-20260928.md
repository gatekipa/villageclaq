## Plain-language activation model

Adding a person creates a group membership first. That person can remain a regular offline member and keep dues, attendance, hosting, and record history without a login. Account activation is a separate, optional step: an authorized officer creates or resends a scoped expiring invitation, or copies its link/code for manual sharing. The person signs in or creates an account whose verified identity matches the invitation. A successful claim attaches that account to the existing membership row; it does not create another member or grant access to another group.
