# Founder role and continuity amendment (2026-10-10)

This owner direction supplements frozen S0-001 (active authorized membership), H-004 (explicit role grants), and G-011/G-012 (independent member transparency and authoritative aggregates). It does not create or renumber a milestone.

1. **Authority:** The active group owner may appoint multiple admins and may create, grant or revoke position permissions. An executive title, `settings.manage`, `roles.manage`, or an officer's own assignment never grants authority to escalate their role. Every consequential grant uses a server check and audit.
2. **Continuity:** The current owner transfers to an active successor's own account through an atomic audited command. The last active owner cannot exit, be removed, or demoted through ordinary member editing. Owner unavailability needs a separately approved verified recovery process; inactivity or a single unverified claim never transfers control.
3. **Departure:** Retain membership and historical authorship, debts, receipts, attendance, minutes and audit records. End effective officer authority immediately; end assignment holder slots without deleting history; hand unfinished work to a new active holder. Former obligations stay reportable, while future recurring dues stop under the active-member producer rule.
4. **Visibility:** Group member count, financial totals and collection rate, financial summary, and shared minutes are independent in-group sharing controls. Personal records remain available under personal RLS. Sharing read-only aggregates never grants receipt, contact, ballot, attachment or mutation access. HQ/public audience settings stay separate.
5. **Verification:** Owner, delegated officer, ordinary member, revoked officer and foreign-group direct requests govern qualification. Open views refresh within the documented 15-second poll plus focus refresh. A hidden button alone is insufficient evidence.

Implementation and unresolved approvals are tracked in [role-visibility-repair-20261010.md](role-visibility-repair-20261010.md) and BUILD_STATUS.
